Applied Case Study

Root-cause analysis found churn concentrated in the first 90 days — driven by fragmented early-life customer experience, not pricing or network quality as leadership assumed. Projected $1–2M in preserved annual revenue and a 20–35% cut in early-life churn.

Client Engagement

The Fix Wasn't More Headcount. It Was Visibility.

Leadership assumed slower performance meant the fix was more staff and capital. The real gap was work-sequencing, not staffing. Built the integrated KPI governance and visibility layer that unlocked $3M in annual savings — without adding headcount or capital.

Onboarding Exercise

The Fix Wasn't Working Faster. But Working Differently.

The bay and crew were capable of far more than 62 minutes — the fix was separating internal work from external. Modeled cycle time dropped to 19.5 minutes, backed by the contribution-margin case behind the redesign.

Applied Case Study
